How It Works

Three steps to produce reliable real estate predictions

01

Continuous Scraping

Real-time data aggregation directly from Mubawab.ma listings, cataloging over 273+ active neighborhoods to capture the latest market dynamics.

02

XGBoost Modelling

Machine learning models optimized for regional property configurations, evaluating surface area, bedroom/bathroom ratios, and amenities.

03

Yakeey Calibration

The resulting predictions are cross-referenced and calibrated against official Yakeey district reference registers, securing a targeted 92% validation confidence.
